# Ethics and Meaning — keeping the boundary clean

This lab models physics and biochemistry. It does not model — and must never claim to
model — the worth of a person, the truth of a faith, the rightness of a politics, or the
cure of a wound. This document keeps those layers separate **so that the science stays
honest and the human meaning stays respected.** One is not evidence for the other.

## The layers, named and separated

| Layer | What it is | What the lab may do | What the lab may NOT do |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Physical** | gravity, radiation, heat | compute, predict, falsify | claim a new force, claim unlimited energy |
| **Biological** | bioenergetics, viability | model proton gradients, aerobic/anaerobic cells | claim oxygen "is" life, claim to define all life |
| **Computational analogy** | agents, priors, precision | simulate belief updating | claim the simulation is reality |
| **Active-inference metaphor** | "miscalibrated signals" | frame model comparison | claim it proves a physical mechanism |
| **Social / political aspiration** | peace, safe water, shelter | record as values | claim math alone achieves them |
| **Spiritual / existential** | "proton as soul," continuity | preserve as metaphor | claim it as established physics |
| **Engineering goal** | better collectors, cleaner water | bound by thermodynamics | promise a breakthrough without device-level proof |

## On trauma and "trauma-locked agents"

The simulation may represent agents that fail to update their beliefs — held in maladaptive
priors or miscalibrated precision across generations. This is a **computational analogy**,
useful for visualising how trust, safety, and evidence access might change a trajectory.

It is **not** a clinical model. The lab makes:

- **no diagnosis** of any person,
- **no claim to cure** trauma,
- **no claim that mathematics alone resolves suffering**,
- **no claim that a simulation guarantees peace**,
- **no manipulative religious or political framing.**

The honest, allowed statement is this: *a bounded active-inference model can illustrate how
belief updating, resource access, safety, and cooperative policies may shift outcomes. It is
a model, not a cure and not a proof.*

## On the human aspirations in the source material

The vision behind this work — less suffering, safe water, energy abundance, education,
shelter, peace, the search for where we came from — is recorded here **as values, held with
respect.** Values do not require empirical warrant and lose nothing by lacking one. The
lab's job is not to validate the dream as physics, nor to diminish it, but to keep the
boundary clean: metaphor honored as metaphor, mechanism reserved for what a measurement can
show. "People must see to learn, and learn to see" — and what they are shown here is exactly
what the math supports, with every fence in plain view.

## On the cross-cultural breadth of the source frame

The source material reaches across many human contexts and traditions. The lab takes no
position on any of them. It neither endorses nor refutes a religion, an ideology, or a
culture. Where the source uses the language of one tradition as metaphor, the lab records it
as metaphor from that tradition, with the same discipline applied to all — no hierarchy, no
ridicule, no appropriation as evidence.
