# Proof Document — Chemiosmosis, the Proton-Motive Force, and the Contingency of Oxygen

## Scope

This document establishes the bioenergetic tier of the decomposed "Ozone = Life" thesis. It proves, within a bounded model, three things: (1) the **proton-motive force** (Δp) is the foundational, near-universal energy-coupling currency of cellular life (Mitchell chemiosmotic theory, Nobel 1978); (2) molecular oxygen is a thermodynamically *powerful* but **not universal** terminal electron acceptor; (3) ozone (O₃) has **no role** in any known cellular bioenergetic pathway. The net result: the bioenergetic tier offers **no support** for "Ozone = Life," and only narrow, oxygen-agnostic support for "proton gradients are foundational to life."

## Equations (preserve exactly)

**Proton-motive force:**

$$\Delta p = \Delta\psi - \frac{2.303\,R\,T}{F}\,\Delta\mathrm{pH}$$

**Free-energy proxy (charge transport through the gradient):**

$$\Delta G \approx -\,n\,F\,\Delta p$$

## Variable + Unit Definitions

| Symbol | Meaning | Units |
|---|---|---|
| Δp | proton-motive force | mV (or V) |
| Δψ | transmembrane electrical potential (inside-negative) | mV |
| ΔpH | pH_in − pH_out | dimensionless |
| 2.303RT/F | Nernst factor (≈59.16 mV/pH @ 298.15 K; ≈61.5 mV/pH @ 310 K) | mV/pH |
| R | molar gas constant = 8.314462618 (exact, 2019 SI) | J·mol⁻¹·K⁻¹ |
| T | absolute temperature | K |
| F | Faraday constant = 96485.33212 (exact, 2019 SI) | C·mol⁻¹ |
| n | protons translocated per ATP (system-dependent, c-ring 8–15) | dimensionless |
| ΔG | electrochemical work available per mole of protons | J·mol⁻¹ |

## Worked Example (with numbers)

**Nernst factor** at T = 298.15 K: 2.303 × 8.314462618 × 298.15 / 96485.33212 = **0.059157 V = 59.16 mV/pH** [Class A]. At physiological 310 K this rises to **61.51 mV/pH** — always declare T.

**Δp**, respiring mitochondrion, Δψ = 160 mV (inside-negative), matrix alkaline by ΔpH = 0.75. With the matrix-alkaline convention the pH term **adds**:

Δp = 160 + (59.16 × 0.75) = 160 + 44.4 = **≈204 mV** (within the reported physiological 180–220 mV range).

**ΔG proxy**, n = 3 protons/ATP at Δp = 0.20 V:

|ΔG| = 3 × 96485 × 0.20 = **57 891 J/mol ≈ 57.9 kJ/mol** — approximately matching the ~50–60 kJ/mol cellular cost of ATP synthesis, consistent with observed ATP-synthase stoichiometry.

## Dimensional Check

**Δp:** Δψ is in V (J/C). Second term: [J·mol⁻¹·K⁻¹ × K ÷ (C·mol⁻¹)] × [dimensionless] = J/C = V. Both terms volts → **consistent**.

**ΔG:** n [dimensionless] × F [C/mol] × Δp [V = J/C] = **J/mol** → **consistent**.

## Evidence Class Per Claim

- Chemiosmotic theory / Δp is foundational coupling currency — **B** (near-A); Mitchell, Nobel 1978.
- Δp equation and 59.16 mV/pH factor — **A** (derived; T-dependent).
- O₂/H₂O couple E°′ ≈ +0.82 V (precise ~+0.815 V) is a *thermodynamic-advantage* fact, **not** a necessity claim — **B**.
- Chemiosmosis runs identically on O₂/sulfate/nitrate/fumarate/CO₂/light (O₂-independent) — **B** (corrected from D per verdict).
- Life predates atmospheric O₂/O₃ (earliest life ~3.5–3.8 Ga; GOE ~2.4 Ga) — **B**.
- ΔG proxy with single n → fixed universal ATP yield — **C** (n is c-ring/transport-dependent; never quote a universal yield).
- "Proton gradients, not O₂/O₃, are the bioenergetic invariant" (framing bridge) — **D**.

## Falsification Conditions

- **"Life requires O₂"** is **contradicted-by-test (X)**: anaerobic respiration (sulfate, nitrate, Fe(III), CO₂, fumarate reducers) and fermentation sustain life with no O₂; life preceded atmospheric O₂ by ~1–1.4 Gyr.
- The chemiosmotic claim would be falsified if a cell were shown to synthesize ATP via the F-ATPase **without** a transmembrane ion-motive gradient — not observed.
- The "proton gradients are foundational" claim **survives-as-narrowed-hypothesis**: oxygen-agnostic, far narrower than "is life itself."
