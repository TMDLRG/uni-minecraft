**Hook (first 220 chars, before "see more"):**
We did not create the world we move through. The discipline of looking — gently, stubbornly,
honestly — is how we honor any big idea. A bounded science film, with Marathi captions.

**Body:**
This is Segment 1 of a five-part film about how to test what we believe. The arc is the
human journey: a curious mind, a bold claim, and the test that decides. Every number on
screen was recomputed from public sources and cross-checked against the code that drew the
visuals. No claim is called "proven."

Three things we tried not to do:
• Promise a breakthrough where the evidence didn't give one.
• Make any verdict feel cruel.
• Pad runtime — this segment is 1 m 40 s of dense, honest narration.

If you stay through the next four acts, you'll see one widely-believed idea survive
(narrowly), one widely-believed idea contradicted, and the cleanest way I know to be wrong
gracefully — clean up the spill, take a new sample, and look again.

Built on the SP.Lab open code (Elixir, zero-dep, deterministic). Reproducible end-to-end.
