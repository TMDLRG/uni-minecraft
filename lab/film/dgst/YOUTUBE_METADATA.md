## DESCRIPTION (one block — paste as-is; ~2.6k of 5k chars)

TRAVELERS is a bounded, honest science film about three bold ideas — that ozone is the source of
life, that the weight you feel is air pressure rather than gravity, and that a single thread runs
through every living thing — and the gentle, stubborn discipline we use to test any idea fairly.

Every number on screen traces to a public source (NASA, ESA, JPL, CODATA) and is re-derived by open
code. No claim is ever called "proven." Some ideas survive the testing, some are contradicted, and
some are preserved as metaphor — each one honestly labeled. Marathi (Devanagari) captions throughout.

Verdict: 5 supported · 2 narrowed hypotheses · 2 preserved metaphors · 3 contradicted · 0 "proven."

भी विचार को निष्पक्षता से परखने के लिए हम जो सौम्य, अडिग अनुशासन अपनाते हैं।
